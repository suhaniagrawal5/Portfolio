Olist Supply Chain Analytics

Problem
An e-commerce marketplace needs to see where deliveries slip, what customers say about them and how much stock to hold.
What I built
An analysis of about 10,000 orders, an app that flags late suppliers and exports results to CSV, and a dashboard for sales, product description insights and delivery status. Portuguese reviews are translated to English for sentiment analysis.
Outcome
Machine-learning demand forecasts turned into recommended inventory stock levels by product category.
SQL Python Excel Machine learning Sentiment analysis

AI Legal Analyzer

Problem
Legal documents are long and dense, and it is easy to miss the clauses that carry risk.
What I built
A tool that accepts pasted text or an uploaded file, summarizes clauses, flags high-risk ones and suggests follow-up questions. A chatbot with guardrails tailored to legal queries sits alongside it.
Outcome
A working AI app, integrated through a secure API key, that keeps answers on topic and points readers to what needs a closer look.
API integration Guardrails AI chatbot
